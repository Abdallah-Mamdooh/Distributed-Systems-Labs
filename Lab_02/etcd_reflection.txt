etcd / Raft Reflection:

I used etcdctl to write and read a key-value pair successfully.

The endpoint status command showed that the etcd node was the leader.

When I stopped the etcd container, the service became unavailable because this setup used only one etcd node.

After restarting etcd, the node became leader again and the Raft term increased from 2 to 3.

In a real multi-node Raft cluster, if the leader fails, the remaining nodes elect a new leader automatically.
