Lab 4 Reflection

1. Microservices Benefits:
Microservices allow independent development, deployment, and scaling of services.

2. Complexity:
Splitting the system introduces communication between services and possible failures.

3. Service Dependency:
Order-service depends on product-service, so failure in one affects the other.

4. Network Latency:
High latency can slow down communication between services.

5. 12-Factor Principles:
- Use of environment variables
- Stateless design
- Port-based communication

6. Failure Handling:
When product-service is down, order-service returns an error, showing how failures propagate.
